§ 06 / FAQ
Will this replace my lawyers?

No. It takes the grunt work off their plate (first drafts, research triage, document review) so they spend their time on judgment instead of busywork.

Is it confidential?

Yes, by design. I work under NDA and build so that confidential data stays under your control: local models, anonymization, and private deployments where needed.

Am I locked into a tool or vendor?

Never. I'm independent and tool-agnostic. You own everything I build (code, prompts, docs) and can run it without me.

Do you give legal advice on my matters?

No. I'm a qualified lawyer, which is why I understand your work, but my job here is to make your team faster and build your tools, not to advise on your cases.

We already use Harvey or CoCounsel. Still useful?

Often more so. I help you get real value out of what you already pay for, and build the pieces those tools don't cover.

Remote or on-site?

Remote worldwide. On-site possible in the EU.

What languages do you work in?

English and Russian.

How much does it cost?

Every engagement is scoped and fixed-price upfront. We settle the number on the intro call, no surprise invoices.
