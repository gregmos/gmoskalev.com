Ymnky manager — a private Telegram bot and board for one band. Last updated 10 October 2026.

Ymnky manager is a private tool run by Grigorii Moskalev for the members of one music band and the guests they invite. It is not offered to the public, and access can be given or removed by the band's coordinator.

Use

The app keeps the band's tasks and, when the band's Google account is connected, creates and changes meetings in that account's Google Calendar on members' requests. Use it only for the band's own work, and only connect a Google account you are allowed to manage.
